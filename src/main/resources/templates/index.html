IST → EST converter

Converts real India time to real US Eastern time, switching between EST and EDT automatically based on the date — a proper time-zone conversion, unlike the flat-offset converters above.

Note: this uses a flat offset (default 4 hours). If the platform follows real US Eastern clock time rather than fixed EST, the correct offset is 4 hours during Daylight Saving (roughly March–November) and 5 hours during Standard Time (roughly November–March). Adjust the offset field above if needed.
